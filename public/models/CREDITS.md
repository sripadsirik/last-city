# Last City architecture assets

`urban-facade.glb` contains the 1K glTF version of **Modular Urban Apartments Facade** by Poly Haven, packaged with its original textures into a self-contained GLB.

- Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/modular_urban_apartments_facade
- License: CC0 1.0 (public domain): https://polyhaven.com/license
- Rebuild: `node scripts/prepare-city-assets.mjs`
- The packaging script verifies source MD5 checksums before writing the asset.

Last City instances selected wall, window and cornice components across four Southbank buildings. The other buildings, hospital, bridges, port, vehicles and residents are generated by the project's own Three.js model builders. The environment is fictional, with compressed distances and animation time; it is not a surveyed city or engineering model.
